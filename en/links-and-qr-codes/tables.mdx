---
title: Tables & QR codes
description: Give each table its own menu link and QR code.
---

Open **Settings** > **Links & QR codes**, then go to **Tables**.

Each table receives a unique menu link and QR code. This lets Mutfaaq identify which table a guest used to open the menu.

## Create tables

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Create Table">
    Choose how many tables you want to create, from 1 to 100 at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the tables">
    For one table, enter its full name. For several tables, enter a prefix such as **Table** and check the preview of the numbered names.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the tables">
    Select **Create Table** or **Create Tables**. You can use **Undo** immediately after creation if needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage a table

From the table list, you can:

- copy its unique menu link;
- download its QR code as SVG or PDF;
- rename it;
- delete it.

Renaming a table does not change its link or the text printed on physical stands already ordered.

<Warning>
  Deleting a table permanently disables its link and every existing QR code connected to it, including QR stands already ordered.
</Warning>

You can select several tables to delete them together, or use **Download all QR codes** to retrieve every table QR code at once.
