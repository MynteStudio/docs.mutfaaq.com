---
title: Public menu link
description: Create and share the generic link for your venue's public menu.
---

Open **Settings**, then select **Links & QR codes**.

The **Generic venue link** opens the public menu without associating the visit with a specific table. Use it on your website, social media, or any other online channel.

## Create the address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter a public menu address">
    Under **Public menu address**, enter a short address based on your venue name.

    Use only lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens, with a maximum of 80 characters. For example: `olive-kitchen`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the link">
    Select **Save menu link**. If the address is already used by another venue, choose a different one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the public menu">
    Select **Open menu** to verify the page your guests will see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share the link

Select **Copy link**, then paste it into your website, social profiles, messages, or other customer communications.

You can also select **Download QR code** to download a generic QR code that opens the same link.

<Note>
  The generic link always opens the menu currently visible to guests for this venue.
</Note>
