---
title: QR stands
description: Order physical QR supports assigned to your tables.
---

Mutfaaq lets you order physical QR supports, including QR stickers and NFC & QR cards. The products, prices, minimum quantities, and estimated delivery times currently available appear directly on the order page.

You must create at least one table before placing an order.

## Prepare an order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the order page">
    Open **Settings** > **Links & QR codes**, then select **Order physical QR stands**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the products and quantities">
    Select the number of each support you need. Check the minimum quantity and estimated total shown on the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign every support to a table">
    Distribute the ordered quantity between your tables. You can assign several copies to the same table or select **Distribute evenly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the printed text">
    Enter the exact text to print for each table. A short value such as **01** or **T01** is recommended.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    When every support is assigned and labelled, select **Continue to secure checkout**. Enter the delivery information during checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Order history

Confirmed payments appear under **Order history**. You can review the order date, quantity, amount, and open the payment receipt when available.

<Warning>
  A physical stand uses the link of its assigned table. Deleting that table permanently stops the stand's QR code from working.
</Warning>
