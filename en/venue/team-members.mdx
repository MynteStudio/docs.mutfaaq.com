---
title: Team members
description: Invite Managers and control who can access the selected venue.
---

Open **Settings**, then select **Team** to view the people who can access the currently selected venue.

## Invite a Manager

Only the Owner can invite a Manager.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the Manager's email address">
    In **Invite a Manager**, enter the email address the person will use for their Mutfaaq account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the invitation language">
    Select the language Mutfaaq should use for the invitation email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Select **Send invitation**. The person does not receive access until they review and accept it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  An address already associated with an Owner or Admin account cannot join as Staff. A person who is already a member or has a pending invitation cannot be invited again.
</Note>

## Pending invitations

An invitation remains under **Pending invitations** until it is accepted, declined, canceled, or expires.

From this section, the Owner can:

- select **Resend** to send the invitation email again;
- select **Cancel invitation** to make the current invitation unusable;
- check its expiration date.

## Manage current members

The **Members** section shows the Owner and every Manager who currently has access to the venue.

The Owner can select **Remove** next to a Manager. The Manager immediately loses access to that venue, but the venue's menus and other data are not deleted.

## Leave a venue

A Manager can select **Leave Venue** next to their own account. Access is removed immediately, and an Owner must send a new invitation if the Manager needs to return.
