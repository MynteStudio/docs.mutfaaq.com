---
title: Roles & permissions
description: Understand what Owners and Managers can do for a venue.
---

Mutfaaq currently provides two levels of access for venue teams: **Owner** and **Manager**.

## Owner

The Owner is responsible for the venue and has full access to its administration.

An Owner can:

- create and manage venues;
- manage menus, products, availability, promotions, tables, and QR codes;
- update venue information and branding;
- invite or remove Managers;
- manage subscriptions and billing;
- delete a venue;
- import or link menus between venues they own.

Each venue has exactly one Owner. An Owner can own several venues.

## Manager

A Manager is invited by an Owner to help operate a specific venue.

A Manager can:

- manage menus, categories, products, options, availability, and promotions;
- update venue information and branding;
- create and manage tables and QR codes;
- view the venue team;
- leave the venue when they no longer need access.

A Manager cannot manage billing, invite Managers, delete the venue, transfer its ownership, or create venues as an Owner.

## Permissions at a glance

| Action | Owner | Manager |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| Manage menus and products | Yes | Yes |
| Manage venue information | Yes | Yes |
| Manage tables and QR codes | Yes | Yes |
| View the team | Yes | Yes |
| Invite Managers | Yes | No |
| Manage billing | Yes | No |
| Delete the venue | Yes | No |
