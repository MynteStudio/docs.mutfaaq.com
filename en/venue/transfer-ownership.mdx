---
title: Transfer ownership
description: Understand how a venue is transferred to a new Owner.
---

An ownership transfer changes the Owner of a venue without deleting its menus or other venue data.

## Request a transfer

To transfer one or more venues, email [support@mutfaaq.com](mailto:support@mutfaaq.com) and provide:

- the venue or venues to transfer;
- the new Owner's email address.

The new Owner does not need to have a Mutfaaq account before the transfer.

<Warning>
  You can transfer ownership only to an email address that is not associated with a Mutfaaq account or to an existing Owner account. You cannot transfer ownership to a Staff account.
</Warning>

## After the transfer

- the recipient becomes the venue's new Owner;
- the previous Owner loses ownership of that venue;
- the venue, its menus, and its associated data remain in place;
- existing Managers remain attached to the venue.

<Note>
  Ownership is transferred only for the venues specified in your request. Other venues belonging to the previous Owner are not affected.
</Note>
