---
title: Venue settings
description: Update the public information and visual identity of your venue.
---

Open **Settings**, then select **Venue** to manage the information customers see on your menus and public pages.

## Logo and banner

In **Brand assets**, you can add, replace, reposition, or remove your venue's images.

- **Logo:** use a square image. A size of **480 × 480 px** is recommended.
- **Banner:** use a wide image with a **3:1 ratio**. A size of **1600 × 534 px** is recommended.

Changes to the logo and banner are saved automatically.

## Venue information

You can update the following public information:

- **Venue name**;
- **Description**;
- **Google Review URL**.

After making your changes, select **Save information**.

### Google Review URL

If you created the venue using Google search, Mutfaaq automatically retrieves its direct Google review link. If you entered the venue manually, you can add the link yourself.

You can also replace the existing link when needed. Make sure it belongs to the currently selected venue before saving it.

## Fixed information

The following settings were chosen when the venue was created and cannot be edited from **Venue settings**:

- **Country**;
- **Venue language**;
- **Reference currency**;
- **Timezone**.

These settings control important behavior such as menu prices, automatic translations, scheduled availability, and promotions.

## Delete a venue

The **Danger zone** lets you schedule the permanent deletion of the venue and its associated data.

<Warning>
  Only schedule deletion when you are certain that the venue and its data are no longer needed. If a deletion is already scheduled, you can cancel it before it takes effect.
</Warning>
