title: Pricing & Limits
description: Understand the free trial, language availability, and venue limits.
---

Mutfaaq includes a 14-day free trial so you can set up your restaurants and test the customer experience before subscribing.

## Your free trial

The trial is available once per Owner. It starts when you create your first restaurant.

- The trial lasts **14 days**.
- Up to **3 restaurants** can use the same trial.
- Restaurants added during the trial share the original trial end date.
- Adding or deleting a restaurant does not restart or extend the trial.
- A fourth restaurant does not receive trial access and requires its own subscription.

<Note>
  The trial belongs to the Owner, not to each restaurant. For example, a restaurant added five days after your trial starts receives the nine remaining days.
</Note>

## Languages during the trial

Each restaurant has one **source language**. This is the language you use when writing your menu content.

During onboarding, you also choose **3 priority languages** for translation. During the trial, customers can view the public menu in:

- the restaurant’s source language;
- the 3 priority languages you selected.

The source language is fixed when the restaurant is created. Choose the language in which your team will write and maintain the menu.

After you activate a subscription, all **17 languages** supported by Mutfaaq become available on the public menu.

## Translation usage limits

Automatic translations include a high monthly usage allowance. These limits are designed to cover normal restaurant use while preventing automated or excessive abuse of the translation service.

## Limits after subscribing

The Starter plan does not limit the number of menus, products, languages, or tables in a restaurant.

Each restaurant has its own subscription. You can see the current price and choose monthly or yearly billing from the **Subscription** page in Mutfaaq.
