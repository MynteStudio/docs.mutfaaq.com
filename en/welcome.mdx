Mutfaaq helps you create a digital menu that your customers can open by scanning a QR code.

You do not need any technical knowledge. This documentation guides you through each task, one step at a time.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your restaurant">
    Add the information and visual identity that your customers will see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your menu">
    Organize your categories, then add your products, prices, photos, and options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the result">
    Check how your menu will look to customers before making it available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and share">
    Publish your menu, create your tables, and share their QR codes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Open Mutfaaq" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://app.mutfaaq.com">
  Sign in to start setting up your restaurant.
</Card>
