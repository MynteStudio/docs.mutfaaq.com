---
title: Access & Permissions
description: Understand what Owners and Managers can do in Mutfaaq.
---

Mutfaaq currently provides two levels of access for restaurant teams: **Owner** and **Manager**.

## Owner

The Owner is responsible for the restaurant and has full access to its administration.

An Owner can:

- create and manage restaurants;
- manage menus, products, availability, promotions, tables, and QR codes;
- update restaurant information and branding;
- invite or remove Managers;
- manage subscriptions and billing;
- delete a restaurant or transfer its ownership;
- import or link menus between restaurants they own.

Each restaurant has exactly one Owner. An Owner can own several restaurants.

## Manager

A Manager is invited by an Owner to help operate a specific restaurant.

A Manager can:

- manage menus, categories, products, options, availability, and promotions;
- update restaurant information and branding;
- create and manage tables and QR codes;
- view the restaurant team;
- leave the restaurant when they no longer need access.

A Manager cannot manage billing, invite Managers, delete the restaurant, transfer its ownership, or create restaurants as an Owner.

## Permissions at a glance

| Action | Owner | Manager |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| Manage menus and products | Yes | Yes |
| Manage restaurant information | Yes | Yes |
| Manage tables and QR codes | Yes | Yes |
| View the team | Yes | Yes |
| Invite Managers | Yes | No |
| Manage billing | Yes | No |
| Delete or transfer the restaurant | Yes | No |
