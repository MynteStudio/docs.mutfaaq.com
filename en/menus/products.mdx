---
title: Products
description: Add products and control how they appear on your menu.
---

Products are the dishes, drinks, and other items offered on your menu. Every product belongs to one category and has its own price.

## Create a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Edit menu">
    From the navigation, select **Edit menu**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category">
    Find the category where the product should appear, then select **Add product**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the product information">
    Enter a name and a price. You can also add a description, calories, and a photo.

    Keep **Translate product name** enabled if the name should be translated. Turn it off for a signature dish, brand, or proper name that must remain unchanged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the product">
    Select **Save**. If you are editing the active menu, the product becomes visible to customers immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can drag a product to change its position or move it into another category.

## Add useful product details

A clear description and a good photo help customers understand what they are ordering. You can add:

- a description of up to 2,000 characters;
- one square product photo;
- calories in kilocalories;
- allergens from the list provided by Mutfaaq.

To add allergens, save the product first, then open it from **Edit menu** and update its general information.

<Warning>
  Allergen information is guidance only and does not guarantee the absence of cross-contamination. Make sure the information you provide is accurate for your restaurant.
</Warning>

## Control product visibility and stock

Open a product to manage its availability. The following controls have different purposes:

| Control | What customers see | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Visible to guests** off | The product is completely hidden. | You do not want to offer or display the product for now. |
| **In stock** off | The product remains visible and is marked sold out. | The product is temporarily out of stock. |
| **Limit availability** | The product follows a recurring schedule. | You only offer it on certain days or during certain hours. |

<Tip>
  If a dish sells out during service, turn off **In stock**. Customers can still see that you normally offer it, but they cannot mistake it for an available product.
</Tip>

## Schedule product availability

Enable **Limit availability** to choose the days and, optionally, a start and end time. The schedule uses the restaurant’s timezone and can continue past midnight.

Outside the scheduled period, choose between:

- **Show as unavailable**, which keeps the product visible;
- **Hide product**, which removes it until its next available period.

For example, a lunch special available from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM can remain visible after 3:00 PM as unavailable, or disappear completely until 11:00 AM the next day.

## Add a product promotion

Products support three types of promotion:

- **Percentage off**, such as 20% off;
- **Fixed amount off**, such as 2 USD off;
- **Final price**, such as a promotional price of 10 USD.

A promotion can start immediately or on a chosen date, stop automatically, and follow a weekly schedule. If several promotions could apply, Mutfaaq uses the first applicable promotion in the order you set. Promotions never stack.

## Edit or delete a product

Select a product from **Edit menu** to change its information, availability, promotions, or options.

<Warning>
  Deleting a product is permanent. If an option shares this product’s stock, Mutfaaq asks whether you want to preserve or delete that option before completing the deletion.
</Warning>
